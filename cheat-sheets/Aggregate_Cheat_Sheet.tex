{\huge{\bf \textbf{\texttt{Aggregate} Class Cheat Sheet}}}

\raggedright
\texttt{\m Aggregate(name, exp\_el, exp\_premium, exp\_lr, exp\_en, exp\_attachment, exp\_limit, exp\_years, exp\_rate, sev\_name, sev\_a, sev\_b,} \\
\texttt{\phantom{\m}sev\_mean, sev\_cv, sev\_loc, sev\_scale, sev\_xs, sev\_ps, sev\_wt, sev\_lb, sev\_ub, sev\_conditional, sev\_signed, sev\_reflect,} \\
\texttt{\phantom{\m}sev\_pick\_attachments, sev\_pick\_losses, occ\_reins, occ\_kind, freq\_name, freq\_a, freq\_b, freq\_zm, freq\_p0, freq\_pin\_mean,} \\
\texttt{\phantom{\m}wait\_*${}^{[0]}$, agg\_reins, agg\_kind, reins\_bucket, dsev\_bucket, value\_type, approximate, label, label\_map, note, hints, tags, doc)}

The constructor follows the DecL clauses (exposure, severity, occurrence reinsurance, frequency, aggregate reinsurance, approximate). Usually built via \texttt{build('agg ...')}.
The following tables show all \texttt{\m methods}, and fields or properties (used interchangeably). Comments elucidate the meaning of more obscure entries.

\bigskip \raggedright
{\bf Notes:}

[0]: \texttt{wait\_*} is the renewal (Sparre-Andersen) waiting-time family: \texttt{wait\_name}, \texttt{\_a}, \texttt{\_b}, \texttt{\_mean}, \texttt{\_cv}, \texttt{\_loc}, \texttt{\_scale}, \texttt{\_xs}, \texttt{\_ps}, \texttt{\_wt}, \texttt{\_lb}, \texttt{\_ub}, \texttt{\_conditional}, \texttt{\_attachment}, \texttt{\_limit}. It pairs with \texttt{exp\_years}, never with a frequency clause.

[1]: matches \texttt{Portfolio}. \quad [2]: \texttt{approximate} is the \emph{action}; \texttt{approximation} is the \emph{stored kind} set by the DecL clause.

Abbreviations: gcn $=$ gross (subject), ceded, net; actual $=$ analytic, est $=$ FFT estimate.

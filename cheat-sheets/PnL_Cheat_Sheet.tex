{\huge{\textbf{\texttt{PnL} Class Cheat Sheet}}}

\raggedright
\texttt{\m PnL($*$, name, source, groups=None, role=None, consideration=None, obligation=None, scale=None, result\_name='result',} \\
\texttt{\phantom{\m}label=None, label\_map=None)} \\
Two construction forms: the ledger \texttt{groups=[Group(...), ...]}, and the sugar \texttt{role=/consideration=/obligation=} for exactly one \texttt{Group}. A \texttt{\m Group(label, role, consideration, obligation)} holds \texttt{\m Leg(label, func, bs, is2d)} cash flows. \\

A \texttt{PnL} is a position: one stochastic \texttt{source} plus an ordered ledger of signed \texttt{Group}s. Each group books a \texttt{consideration} and an \texttt{obligation} under a \texttt{role} (\texttt{sell} $\to +$consideration, $-$obligation; \texttt{buy} $\to$ the contra), so the \texttt{EX} column adds straight down the sheet. All derived rows are per-atom partial sums, never arithmetic on marginals, so covariance is carried for free. Build with \texttt{build('pnl ...')} / \texttt{build('xpnl ...')}, or \texttt{\m Aggregate.make\_pnl}.
\texttt{pnl} is the \emph{consolidated} single-group net view, a flat \texttt{Consideration} / \texttt{Obligation} / \texttt{Margin} card; \texttt{xpnl} is the exploded sibling, same syntax, a multi-group walk with one group per step, so \texttt{summary\_df} carries one \texttt{(Step, View)} block per step plus a closing \texttt{'All'}, and \texttt{stats\_df} is three-level \texttt{(Step, View, Line)}.
The following tables show all \texttt{\m methods}, and fields or properties (used interchangeably). Comments elucidate the meaning of more obscure entries.

\texttt{\m evaluate} (the Cherny--Madan breakeven acceptability panel)

\smallskip
{\it A P\&L is} \rm evaluated, not priced\it. You price the \rm obligation\it{} and ask which distortion drives the risk-adjusted net to zero: the breakeven stress the position survives. The breakeven \texttt{gini\_p} is the single family-agnostic acceptability index.

\smallskip{\it Needs one obligation leg over a regular lattice source.}

## D. Cross-cutting semantic facts the IR must carry

1. **Atoms mean no silent binning.** `resolution='full'` is the app default because a discretized aggregate is routinely atomic; any binning merges an atom with its neighbors and no threshold avoids it (`objects.py:1054-1084`). Where a display reduction IS wanted (the joint surface), it must be mass preserving, because the reduction is meaning **[J3]**.
2. **Mass vs ordinate.** `p_total` is a bucket mass; `pdf` is a density ordinate that must never be summed; compositors convert with `/bs` in scattered places. The series role plus the axis `unit` carry this distinction; emitters do the conversion, renderers never do arithmetic.
3. **Survival derivations.** Three conventions live today: server `S` columns, server-synthesized (`sf`, `1-cumsum`), and client-side reins accumulation with the `max(0, .)` clamp. Emitters own all of it; the clamp and the "a ceded survival may legitimately start at 0" fixture note travel with the reins emitter.
4. **Signedness.** A P&L outcome axis is signed and its bad tail is the low end (downside read off F, break-even mark at 0). Axis unit plus series role express this; no renderer branches on object kind.
5. **The float-dust floor.** `LOG_FLOOR = 1e-15` app side vs 1e-12/1e-14/1e-15/1e-16 scattered through the compositors **[J5]**. Values below the floor are arithmetic noise, not tail; on log scales they draw as a fringe that reads as signal.
6. **Series pairing across panels.** The app links legend toggles and cursors by emission-order arithmetic (`linkPanels` `half = total/2`). The IR pairs by series *name* identity across panels, explicitly.
7. **Draw order and emphasis are meaning.** Units first, total last; gross first, net on top. Document order is draw order; the role ('total', 'net') lets a renderer emphasize without a style knob in the IR.
8. **Equal aspect is semantic in exactly the unit-square and complex-plane cases** (distortion, bounds envelope, weight contour, Fourier disk) and wrong elsewhere (anisotropic joint grids are deliberately `auto`). Panel-level field.
9. **Per-axis grids differ on bivariate objects** (independent components, different `bs`): a z grid is two vectors plus a matrix, never one step. Long-form, not aligned-wide, is the compute-side truth (`bivariate_marginal_frame` rationale).
10. **Axis-name recovery.** The joint frame payload is name-lossy on axis 1 (values become column headers); the app re-derives the name from `stats_df`. Emitters ship both resolved component labels and end that hack.

## E. Judgment calls for the author

1. **Twin return-period axis: IR or renderer trick?** The pairing T = 1/S is statistical meaning and only aligns against a log survival axis; the app encodes it as `twin` state. *Recommendation: IR.* `ChartAxis.reciprocal_of` names the paired axis; renderers realize it as a twin (drafted into the schema, remove if vetoed).
2. **Reference-line toggles: view state or IR?** The app's `refLines` toggle shows/hides mean and anchors. *Recommendation: marks are always IR (they carry meaning: which anchors, at which values, from `tail_df` authority, faint or full weight, label sides); their visibility toggle is renderer view state.* The reins chart simply has no tail/mean marks emitted.
3. **Display-grid binning of the joint surface: emitter-side, and which label convention?** Reduction is mass preserving and semantic, so it moves into the emitter. But the app labels reduced cells at block right edges while the server's 1-D `bin_density` labels at node centers. *Recommendation: adopt the app's right-edge convention for the surface emitter so the pilot's ECharts-option diff is clean, and record the center-vs-edge divergence as a post-pilot reconciliation item.*
4. **The surface default camera (alpha 24, beta 40, looking down the diagonal) is chosen to reveal the dependence ridge.** *Recommendation: keep camera renderer-side (in the per-chart override dict) but preserve the angle; it is a defensible exception either way.*
5. **One log floor.** *Recommendation: standardize on 1e-15 (matches the app's LOG_FLOOR and `_aggregate.py`'s panel B) as the emitters' gap floor; the mpl compositors converge on it chart by chart during conversions, under the image gate.*
6. **Bands.** The margin panels' labeled fill between two curves (and the bounds envelope) is a semantic object. *Recommendation: `ChartSeries.y2` for band roles (drafted into the schema; the 8-chart scope does not use it, plot_twelve representability does).*

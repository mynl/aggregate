1 · An aggregate

agg LABEL VOLUME COVERAGE SEVERITY FREQUENCY
agg Book                  # label
  250 claims              # volume
  1000 xs 0               # coverage
  sev lognorm 100 cv 1.5  # severity
  poisson                 # frequency

Clauses in this order, coverage optional.

7 · A portfolio

port Two.Books
  agg Book 250 claims 1000 xs 0
    sev lognorm 100 cv 1.5 poisson
  agg Casualty 20 claims 2000 xs 0
    sev lognorm 250 cv 2 mixed gamma 0.4

Units are independent, share one grid, and sum to the total. A unit is any agg, with its own reinsurance, or a stored one such as agg.BasicBook. Pricing allocates by unit.

9 · Writing rules

  • A blank line, or ; at the end of a line, separates statements.
  • Line breaks and indentation are free. # comment
  • Labels start with a letter; dots group them: Book.Net.
  • 10_000 65% inf; arithmetic within parentheses, (3 * exp(4) / 2).
  • Lists [1 2 3][1:6][2:12:2].
  • Trailer, last: note{...}tags{...}hints{log2=16;}.

10 · The library, and more

  • agg.BasicBook, port.TwoLineBook reuse shipped objects; Ctrl+K searches them.
  • distortion D wang 0.3 a pricing risk measure.
  • bivariateclash two dependent lines.
  • grossnet agg ... gross and net as one joint view.
  • nttagg exact answers for small discrete books.
  • chfagg S 2 * stable 1.5 1 + 10 a law from its characteristic function, shapes only, scaled and shifted as a severity is: stable, tweedie, norm, gamma, invgauss and more.
  • tweedie 1.5 100 2 converts between Tweedie parameterizations.
  • stable 1000 claims sev 100 * lomax 1.5 the stable limit of a Pareto book.
  • stable x 1.5 0.85 names a stable law, referenced as stable.x; tweedie t 1.5 100 2 likewise.
  • stable.x + 2 * stable.y + 3 combines named laws with a common alpha, each reference an independent copy.
  • Everything else: the DecL reference.
